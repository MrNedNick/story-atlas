In 2005 Kazakhstan completed the **Kok-Aral dam**, which keeps the Syr Darya's water in
the North Aral instead of letting it spill south. The northern sea rose, became less salty,
and fishing slowly returned.

The South Aral had no such help. In 2014 satellite images showed its eastern basin
completely dry for the first time in modern history.

The same sea, the same decades, and two opposite outcomes — decided mostly by where the
remaining water was allowed to go.
