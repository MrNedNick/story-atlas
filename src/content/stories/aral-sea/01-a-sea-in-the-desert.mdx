The Aral Sea sits in the desert between Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan, far from any ocean.
It has no outlet: water arrives through two rivers, the **Amu Darya** from the south and
the **Syr Darya** from the east, and leaves only by evaporation.

For most of the twentieth century that balance held. Around 1960 the Aral was one of the
largest lakes on Earth by surface area, with fishing towns on its shores and ports that
shipped fish across the Soviet Union.
